## Projects

### 1. Measles Vaccination Coverage Among Children Aged 12–23 Months in Nigeria

A descriptive analysis of measles vaccination coverage among children aged 12–23 months using Nigerian survey microdata.

The project examines vaccination coverage overall and across selected demographic and socioeconomic characteristics, including:

* Household wealth
* Maternal education
* Geopolitical zone
* Child sex
* Child age

[View the Measles Vaccination Project](01_Measles-Vaccination-Nigeria)
